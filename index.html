How we are building it

One server (cpu160, University of Waterloo), local disk, lake build at slightly lowered priority because the machine is shared. The open-file limit is raised to the hard limit (the first attempt failed while unpacking the Mathlib cache with the default limit of 1024). Kernel replay runs lake env leanchecker on each of the project's modules, 48 at a time. Every result on this page comes from our own runs.

What a successful check will and won't mean

If the compile succeeds, the axioms are only the standard three, and the kernel accepts every module, then the five theorems are proved, assuming the Lean kernel is sound and Mathlib's definitions of ζ and of Dirichlet L-functions mean what they are supposed to. It says nothing about whether the shortened proof is the clearest one, or how its arguments compare with the original; we are studying that separately.
